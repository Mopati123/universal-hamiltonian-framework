This edition separates established mathematics and physics from engineering analogies, research hypotheses, and empirically validated claims.

## Claim classes

- **standard_physics** — established mathematics or physics within stated assumptions.
- **engineering_analogy** — a deliberately constructed computational or systems analogy.
- **research_hypothesis** — a proposal that still requires theoretical or empirical support.
- **empirically_validated** — a scoped claim backed by reproducible evidence under a declared protocol.

A chapter may use more than one class when it explicitly separates them.

## Book-level invariants

The Book is valid only if all of the following remain true:

1. Hamiltonian flow is not described as generic energy minimization.
2. Gradient flow, dissipative flow, and soft-score optimization remain separate concepts.
3. Classical coupling is not called quantum entanglement.
4. Inverted potentials or unstable modes are not presented as proof of faster-than-light or retrocausal signaling.
5. Generated AI narration is not presented as measurement of subjective experience.
6. Custom integration scores are not called IIT Φ unless the IIT formalism is actually implemented.
7. Cryptographic security is not inferred from chaos, thermodynamic cost, or quantum terminology alone.
8. Market analogies are not promoted to literal physical laws.
9. Trading performance claims require out-of-sample data, realistic costs, leakage controls, baselines, and reproducibility.
10. Scientific models remain revisable in response to evidence.
11. Governance invariants can change only through explicit authorized governance procedures.
12. Every executable example advertised as runnable must be exercised in CI; incomplete examples must be labeled pseudocode.
13. Navigation and chapter counts derive from the canonical manifest.
14. Documentation, implementation, tests, and evidence must agree before certification.

## Status

This index no longer claims that "everything is literally Hamiltonian," that consciousness has been measured, that retrocausal trading is demonstrated, that cryptography is unbreakable, or that market advantage has been proven.

The intellectual goal of UHF remains broad: use a disciplined common language to compare dynamics, constraints, optimization, evidence, and governed execution across domains while preserving the boundary between established science, engineering abstraction, hypothesis, and empirical validation.
